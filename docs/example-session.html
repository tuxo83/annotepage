A detailed usage example

The three sentences of how you use it, at length. One page under review, one remark, one turn — shown, not described.

Your project

The key is in the tag, or it is not and each reviewer pastes it once.

When your fix goes live

Whether the fix is already online the next time the reviewer opens the page, or still waiting behind a deploy.

They change what the steps below show. Neither asks you to choose anything: you are already in one of these cases.

  1. She pastes the project key

    Once, in her browser. Without it this browser can neither read the notes nor write any.

  2. Somebody annotates the page

    They press the button, click what is wrong, and write a sentence about it.

  3. You ask your assistant

    One line names the site. The rest is its own work.

    Your assistant has the key too. You gave it once, in its configuration, when you plugged it in.

  4. The reviewer finds her answer

    She opens the page again and finds what was measured, under the element she pointed at.

    Resolved is not online. The fix reaches her at your next deploy. Until then the remark stays in front of her: the defect is still on her screen.

The terminal session above is an example of a Claude Code session, shortened. The name is used to say what the tool was, not to claim any endorsement.
